Questions

Is WeGetIt live yet?+
Not yet — we’re launching in Dearborn, MI soon. Sign up above and we’ll email you the moment it’s live in your area.
Why start in Dearborn?+
Community density, a market that’s been underserved by the big platforms, and Michigan’s regulatory environment make it the right place to prove the model before expanding nationally.
How is $1 delivery sustainable?+
The math works because we take only enough of what we need from the restaurant, and one dollar from you. No marketing fees, no bloated service fees, no padding.
What’s the Founding Restaurant Program?+
The first restaurants to join lock in reduced or 0% commission forever as we scale. It rewards the partners who took a chance on us early.
How do drivers actually get paid more?+
WeGetIt only profits $1 per order — everything else flows to driver base pay. Lower fees also mean customers have more room to tip generously instead of that money disappearing into platform fees before they ever see the tip screen.
