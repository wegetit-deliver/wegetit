Step 4 — The Comparison
Same Restaurant. Different Platform.
How DoorDash Actually Works
🪝They onboard you at 0% commission for 30-60 days so you get hooked on the order volume.
📈After the trial, you pay 15-30% permanently — with no path to a lower rate, ever.
🚫Auto-refunds issued without calling you. You can't reach your customer to fix the order first.
💸Stack ads, promotions, and hidden fees on top. That's what you just calculated above.
This Is What a Partner Looks Like
WeGetIt doesn't hook you at 0% and then overcharge you once you're dependent. We ask you to pay a higher rate temporarily — because you're carrying this business while we grow. In return, we permanently lower your rate or take it to zero. You carried us. We remember that.

No auto-refunds. Call your customer directly. 0% on pickup orders. Equal placement — no pay-to-rank. No hidden fees. You could be paying 45% all-in right now without knowing it. This isn't a platform relationship. It's a partnership.

Pass Strategy — Revision Flag
⚑ Revisit Post-Launch
If average users order 4x/month or fewer and pass subscription rate stays below 20%, the driver supplement pool stays near $0. This does not kill break-even — WeGetIt still nets $1/order. But it removes the strongest driver retention incentive. Track pass conversion in PostHog from week one. If below 20% after 500 users, push Pass conversion harder before the 10-order free limit. Consider $4.99 intro month.
Commission Language — Key Nuance
The "Nearly Half" Context
WeGetIt's "nearly half" claim only holds against DoorDash Premier (30%). Against Basic (15%) WeGetIt is more expensive in base commission. Against Plus (25%) they're equal. For Basic and Plus restaurants, lead with total true cost — no auto-refunds, no marketing fee, 0% pickup, direct customer access. The 0% FRP permanent tier is the strongest differentiator for all tiers.
